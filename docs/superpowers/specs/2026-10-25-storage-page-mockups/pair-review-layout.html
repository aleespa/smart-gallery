How should reviewing a pair look?

Both live in the desktop app. Click the one closer to what you picture. Comments in the terminal.

A

One pair at a time, big

The two copies fill the screen side by side. Choose one and the next pair appears. A list on the left shows where you are, grouped by folder. Arrow keys pick a side and Enter confirms.

B

A list of pairs, small

Each row is one pair with small previews and Keep buttons. You go down the list choosing. Clicking a row opens it big when you need a closer look.
